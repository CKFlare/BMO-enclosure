# BMO enclosure

BMO enclosure is exactly what it sounds like, it is an enclosure for my week 1 PCB that looks like BMO because the sprite in my Starbie is BMO. I made it because I didn't want my Starbie to be sitting around without a shell, it could be damaged, and it would look ugly so, I made this.

***click image to access public Onshape model**

it is separated into two halves, the top lid and the base container. These are designed to be 3D printed. The two halves go together via a ridge and matching divot to keep them in place and then glue. To mount the PCB threaded inserts must be glued into the standoffs and then the PCB must be screwed on. To attach the screen there are little posts on the lid that it must be slid onto then the screen must be taped to the lid. Finnaly the buttons must simply be pushed onto the switches, they are friction fit and should stay on how it is.
